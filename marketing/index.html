How it works

From a roster to members back in class

  1. 1

    Bring your members in

    Upload a CSV export from Mindbody, Glofox or a spreadsheet. Your roster is saved to your account.

  2. 2

    See who is slipping

    Every member gets a risk score from 0 to 100 based on visit gaps, their usual schedule, bookings, payments and tenure.

  3. 3

    Reach out by text

    AI drafts a personal message for each at-risk member. You review it and send with one tap.

  4. 4

    Handle the reply and follow up

    Replies land in your inbox with an answer already drafted. No reply? A follow-up goes out on the day you set.

Features

Built for the owner who runs the front desk too

Revenue at risk, first thing

The dashboard opens on the dollars at risk this month and a short list of who to reach today, ranked by risk and value.

Risk scores you can read

Red above 75, yellow from 35 to 75, green below 35, with the reasons behind each score in plain words.

Texts that sound like you

Each message is written for that member's situation, not a mail-merge template. Edit anything before it sends.

Two-way replies

When a member texts back, PulseRetain drafts your answer. You approve it, and the whole conversation stays in one place.

Automatic follow-ups

Silence after the first text? A follow-up is scheduled for you and sent within quiet hours in your gym's time zone.

Why members leave

Reasons from member replies roll up into a short list of what's driving cancellations and what to fix first.

Texting done by the rules

Opt-out with STOP is honored automatically, the first text carries the opt-out notice, quiet hours are enforced and daily send limits keep you safe.

You stay in control

Nothing goes to a member without your approval, except the follow-ups you schedule yourself.

Your data stays yours

Each gym's members are kept separate behind its own login, and API keys never reach the browser.

Questions owners ask

Does it work with Mindbody?

Yes, through a member export: download a CSV from Mindbody (or Glofox, or any spreadsheet) and upload it. A direct, automatic Mindbody sync is in development.

Do texts go out without me seeing them?

No. You approve the first text and every reply. The only automatic texts are follow-ups you've scheduled, and you can cancel those any time.

What number do the texts come from?

A PulseRetain business number. Members can reply to it, and their replies show up in your inbox.

What happens when the trial ends?

Pick a plan to keep going. You're only charged if you choose one, since the trial never asks for a card.
